Before You Enroll

Common questions that come up before saying yes

We'd rather answer these now than have them hold you back.

"I've never done bodywork before."

This course was built for beginners. Every technique is demonstrated step by step, starting with your own body.

"I'm not very spiritual."

You don't need to be. This is body-based work grounded in anatomy, breath, and touch. Take what feels right, and leave the rest.

"I don't have trauma to heal."

You don't need a wound to benefit. Most women simply want to feel more connected to their body and at ease in their cycle.

"I've had a hysterectomy."

This work is about far more than one organ. Many women without a uterus still find real value in it for pelvic circulation and tension release.

"I'm postmenopausal. Will this still help me?"

Yes. This course meets you wherever you are, cycle or no cycle. The self-care and massage practices remain just as relevant.

"I'm nervous about touching my own body."

That's completely normal, and it's exactly why we go slowly, one demonstrated sequence at a time.

"I'm too busy for another course."

You have lifetime access, so there's no deadline. Most women start with 10-15 minutes a few times a week.

"I've already done my own healing work."

Then this course meets you at the next step. Many students take it specifically for the techniques and certification to start offering this work to others.
